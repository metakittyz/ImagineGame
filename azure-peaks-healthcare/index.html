Your provider

Dr. Jan Whitehead

Founder & Lead Physician, Whole-Person & Integrative Medicine

Dr. Whitehead built Azure Peaks Healthcare on a simple belief: good medicine takes time, and every patient deserves to be treated as a whole person — not a symptom list. With a background spanning conventional primary care and integrative, natural medicine, she works with patients to build care plans that fit real life, not just a chart.

She holds a deep respect for the land and pace of the Pacific Northwest, and brings that same grounded, unhurried approach into the exam room — listening first, explaining clearly, and treating patients as partners in their own care.

Questions

Good to know

What happens during the free phone consultation?

It's a relaxed, 15-minute call with our care team. We'll talk through what's bringing you in, answer any questions about Dr. Whitehead's approach, and walk you through pricing and support options — with zero obligation to book anything afterward.

Do I need insurance to become a patient?

Not at all. We offer sliding-scale pricing, transparent self-pay rates, and help finding local assistance programs, so lack of insurance is never a reason to skip care.

What conditions do you treat?

Everything from everyday illness and injury to long-term management of chronic conditions, plus preventive care, nutrition and lifestyle support, and integrative therapies — care for the whole person, not just one complaint.

How is this different from a typical doctor's visit?

Visits are longer and unhurried, care plans blend conventional and natural approaches, and Dr. Whitehead treats you as a partner in decisions about your own health — not just a chart to move through.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we're currently welcoming new patients of all ages, with same-week appointments typically available after your free consultation.
